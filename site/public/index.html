Features

Built for people in more than one club

Most tools assume you belong to one club. Readers rarely do. Chapterr keeps each club its own room, and keeps you whole across all of them.

  • Many clubs, one account

    Switch between clubs instantly. Each club keeps its own list, meetings, members and chat, independent of the others.

  • One shelf across clubs

    Your library, goals and reviews travel with you. Share what you choose with each club, and nothing more.

  • Club chat and direct messages

    Realtime conversation for the club, plus private messages between members. Nothing in chat is ever public or shareable.

  • Meetings and reading schedules

    Set the next meeting, assign chapters by date, and let everyone see where the club is. No more scrolling back for the plan.

  • Shared list with an annual goal

    A reading list the whole club can see, in order, with a goal for the year and progress against it.

  • Invite links, not a directory

    People join with a link or code from an admin. There is no public listing of clubs and no way for strangers to find yours.

  • Goodreads import

    Bring your reading history with you. Upload your Goodreads export and your shelves, dates and ratings arrive in your library.

  • Installable, no app store

    Add Chapterr to your home screen from the browser. It opens like an app and the pages you’ve visited work offline.

  • Privacy first

    No public profiles, no ads, no selling data. Nothing leaves your club unless you create an explicit share link, which you can revoke.

How it works

Three steps, then a long time reading

  1. 1

    Create a club

    Give it a name. You’re the owner; you can hand that over later. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    Share the invite link

    Send it to the group however you already talk. Members create an account, land in the club, and that’s it.

  3. 3

    Read together

    Pick the book, set the schedule, open the chat. Each member’s own shelf keeps up in the background.

Questions

Asked often

Is it really free?

Yes. During beta, every club and every feature is free, and we don’t ask for a card. We’re a small team; the beta is how we learn what clubs need before we charge anyone.

What happens after beta?

Each club costs $1 per month, paid by the person who created it. Members never pay. We’ll give 30 days’ notice before billing starts, and we’ll say exactly what happens to clubs that aren’t renewed before anyone is charged.

Can I be in several clubs?

That’s the point. One account can belong to up to 20 clubs and own up to 10. Each club is its own space with its own members, list, meetings and chat; you switch between them from the sidebar.

Is anything public?

No. There are no public profiles and no directory of clubs. Other members see only what you share with a club you both belong to. The one exception is a share link you create yourself, for a review, a quote or a club list; it works until you revoke it. Chats can never be shared.

Can I import from Goodreads?

Yes. Export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file and upload it in Chapterr. Shelves, dates read and ratings come across to your personal library. Imports are deleted from our servers after 30 days.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Chapterr is a web app that installs from the browser on iPhone, Android and desktop, with no app store. Once installed it opens like any other app, and the pages you’ve visited keep working offline.

How do people join?

An admin creates an invite link or code and sends it however the club already talks. Invites can expire, be limited to a number of uses, or be revoked. Nobody can find or join a club without one.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes, both, from Settings. Download your data at any time. Deleting your account removes your personal data; a club owner can delete a club, which removes it for everyone after a 30-day grace period. See the privacy notice for details.
